# <b>Product Management Case Study: Feature Enhancement Plan for Eklipse.gg</b>

---

## <b>1.	Feature Selection: AI Highlights</b>

### <i><b>1.1	Reason for Selection</b></i>

Although themes like <b>App Stability (10)</b>, <b>Monetization (10)</b>, and <b>Platform Integration (8)</b> received a higher number of user complaints compared to <b>AI Highlights (7)</b>, these themes primarily reflect <b>system-leve</b>l or <b>policy-related limitations</b>. Issues like crashes, paywalls, or third-party account connectivity often require long-term infrastructure or business model adjustments beyond the scope of a single feature enhancement. In contrast, <b>AI Highlights </b>is a<b> core, user-facing feature</b> that can be directly improved by the product and engineering teams, with a tangible effect on user experience and retention.

Based on [<b><span color="blue">user reviews on Play Store over the past 3 months</span></b>](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/0/d/1d5MLBKbmXR1XFFz4LUJRhdu3nMRKVkVwOmElsfawVKI/edit), AI Highlights is among the most criticized features (see Appendix A for full theme breakdown). A total of <b>7 negative reviews</b> cited issues such as:

- Inaccurate moment detection (e.g., missed jump scares, irrelevant cuts)
- Irrelevant or mistimed meme overlays
- Excessive processing times
- Final clips often deemed irrelevant or unusable

This consistent pattern suggests a <b>clear gap between user expectations and actual performance</b>, especially for streamers looking for faster highlight automation. Addressing this gap presents a practical opportunity to improve the core product experience.

### <i><b>1.2	Why It Matters</b></i>

AI Highlights plays a central role in <b>Eklipse.gg’s value proposition</b> — <b>automating the time-consuming process of scrubbing hours of footage to find share-worthy content</b>. For many streamers, especially solo creators and small channels, this feature is the key reason they try the platform.

When these shortcomings occur, it directly erodes user trust and satisfaction. Negative feedback on Play Store shows that poor highlight quality not only frustrates users but also discourages them from upgrading to premium or continuing to use the product.

Improving this feature directly impacts:

- <b>User retention</b>, by delivering more usable clips
- <b>Perceived value of premium plans</b>, which depend on highlight quality
- <b>Shareability and growth</b>, as better clips lead to more exposure across platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels

In short, enhancing AI Highlights strengthens the core utility of Eklipse.gg and reinforces its position as a reliable, smart assistant for streamers.

## <b>3. 	Proposed Enhancement</b>

To address the recurring issues found in recent user reviews and product metrics, I propose a targeted enhancement plan focused on <b>four key areas</b>: <b>moment detection</b>, <b>meme overlay logic</b>, <b>processing time</b>, and <b>content preview</b>.

### <i><b>3.1	Targeted Enhancements</b></i>

1. <b>Improve Moment Detection Accuracy</b>

<b>Problem: </b>The AI frequently fails to capture emotionally significant moments such as jump scares, kill streaks, or humorous interactions — especially in horror or narrative-driven games. This leads to low-quality clips that don’t resonate with users.

<b>Enhancement:</b>

- Fine-tune detection models using genre-specific training data (e.g., detect loud audio spikes in horror, rapid kill sequences in FPS).
- Incorporate contextual metadata such as game titles, in-game audio intensity, and viewer chat spikes to improve the AI’s ability to recognize highlight-worthy events.

2. <b>Smarter Meme Overlay & Sound Logic</b>

<b>Problem: </b>Many users report that meme overlays and sound effects are misplaced or irrelevant to the content, often disrupting the emotional tone of the clip.

<b>Enhancement:</b>

- Train a separate tagging model to detect the emotional or contextual tone of a moment and pair it with fitting meme/sound overlays.
- Allow users to customize the presence and intensity of memes through a toggle (e.g., None / Minimal / Full) before finalizing the clip.

3. <b>Processing Time Optimization</b>

<b>Problem: </b>Clip generation can take over 12–24 hours, particularly for long-form streams, making the process slower than manual editing — defeating the core promise of automation.

<b>Enhancement:</b>

- Prioritize processing based on user type (e.g., premium users get faster turnaround), stream length, and user activity levels.
- Use adaptive frame sampling to speed up analysis by skipping low-activity segments and focusing only on parts of the stream with significant in-game action or viewer interaction.
- Introduce early-exit logic to stop processing once a sufficient number of highlight-worthy moments are found to avoid unnecessary computation.

4. <b>Preview & Feedback Loop</b>

<b>Problem: </b>Many users discard AI-generated clips without using them, indicating dissatisfaction and lack of control over final output quality.

<b>Enhancement:</b>

- Introduce a lightweight clip rating system (e.g., thumbs up/down, report issue) to gather structured qualitative feedback.
- Use feedback signals to iteratively retrain the models and personalize future clips based on user preferences.
- Add a preview screen showing AI-chosen moments side-by-side, allowing users to compare and refine clips before export.

### <i><b>3.2	Problem-Solution Fit</b></i>

The enhancements directly align with the top issues raised in recent user reviews (see Appendix B). These include poor moment detection, mistimed meme overlays, long processing times, and unusable clips. By targeting these pain points, the solution ensures strong alignment with user needs and delivers improvements that are both impactful and relevant to the Eklipse user base.

### <i><b>3.3	Scope</b></i>

The proposed improvements are designed to be realistic and achievable within 3 months. They make use of systems that already exist, so the team does not need to rebuild the product from scratch. Each change is meant to create a clear benefit for users without requiring major technical changes behind the scenes.

Specifically, the scope is limited to:

- <b>Model fine-tuning</b>, not full retraining — leveraging existing detection pipelines with improved training data and tagging strategies.
- <b>UI-level additions</b>, such as preview screens and meme toggle controls, which can be incrementally added without disrupting core workflows.
- <b>Backend adjustments</b> like adaptive frame sampling and early-exit logic, applied at the feature layer without reworking the core AI architecture.
- <b>Lightweight feedback mechanisms</b>, such as in-product clip ratings, that require minimal engineering but provide high learning value.

By focusing on these incremental yet impactful changes, the enhancement plan remains realistic in terms of effort, while still driving clear improvements to user experience and perceived product value.

## <b>5. 	Suggestions</b>

To ensure a smooth rollout and strong adoption of the enhanced AI Highlights feature, the following approaches are recommended:

### <i><b>5.1	Help Users Discover What’s New</b></i>

Include in-product guidance to explain the updated features. Tooltips, welcome messages, or short banners can briefly introduce the meme customization toggle, the clip preview interface, and the new clip rating system. These simple cues help users understand the new tools without overwhelming them.

### <i><b>5.2	Make Feedback Rewarding</b></i>

Encourage users to rate the clips they receive by offering small incentives. These could include badges, unlockable themes, or fun summary stats showing their impact. This turns feedback into something users enjoy doing and builds a sense of contribution to improving the product.

### <i><b>5.3	Communicate the Update Clearly</b></i>

Announce the improvements with messaging that focuses on user benefits. Use clear phrases like “Better moment detection, faster processing, more editing control.” Show visual comparisons between the old and new versions of AI Highlights. Include quotes or examples from early users to build trust. Share the message through emails, the website, and community channels.

### <i><b>5.4	Keep Improving After Launch</b></i>

After releasing the feature publicly, continue to monitor how users interact with it. Track metrics like clip deletion rates, engagement levels, and time to process. Run interface tests to explore which layout or controls perform best. Set up monthly team reviews to evaluate user feedback and make model adjustments or UX refinements when needed.
